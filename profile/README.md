# EmpoweredHouse

EmpoweredHouse is a software company in Poznań, Poland, backed by [Appunite](https://appunite.com). We build 2 products: **EHOS**, a governed runtime for software a company owns, and **Empowered Intelligence**, which helps leaders see how their company actually runs.

**Leaders and CTOs:** start at [empowered.house](https://empowered.house). This organization holds the open tools we publish.

---

## For engineers

### [company-os](https://github.com/EmpoweredHouse/company-os)

A starter kit for writing down how a company works: its processes, its rules and the directional decisions behind them, in markdown that people and AI agents can both read. Fork it, fill it in for your own company, and compare notes with others who do the same.

### [accrete](https://github.com/EmpoweredHouse/accrete)

Decision records (SDR, PDR, ADR) as version-controlled knowledge that agents and people can search, so each decision makes the next one easier.

### [process-native-starter](https://github.com/EmpoweredHouse/process-native-starter)

The `pns` CLI. It bootstraps a Phoenix project with an AI engineering harness: an AGENTS.md structure, decision records through accrete, and CI quality gates.

Our default stack is Elixir and Phoenix.
